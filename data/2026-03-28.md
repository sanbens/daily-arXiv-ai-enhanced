### [1] [Integrating Deep RL and Bayesian Inference for ObjectNav in Mobile Robotics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25366)
*João Castelo-Branco,José Santos-Victor,Alexandre Bernardino*

Main category: cs.RO

TL;DR: 该论文提出了一种结合贝叶斯推理和深度强化学习的混合物体搜索框架，旨在提高移动机器人在室内环境中搜索物体时的成功率并减少搜索工作量。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 自主物体搜索对于在室内环境中操作的移动机器人来说是一个挑战，因为存在部分可观测性、感知不确定性以及探索与导航效率之间的权衡问题。传统的概率方法虽然能够明确表示不确定性，但通常依赖于手工制作的动作选择启发式算法；而深度强化学习虽然可以实现自适应策略，但往往收敛速度慢且解释性有限。

Method: 本文提出的混合物体搜索框架将贝叶斯推断与深度强化学习相结合。该方法维护了一个关于目标位置的空间信念图，并通过从校准的对象检测中进行在线贝叶斯推断来更新此图。同时训练一个强化学习策略，直接根据这种概率表示选择导航动作。

Result: 该方法在Habitat 3.0平台上进行了现实室内模拟测试，并与开发出的基线策略进行了比较。在两个室内环境中，所提出的方法提高了成功率同时减少了搜索努力。

Conclusion: 结果表明，结合贝叶斯信念估计与学习型动作选择有助于在部分可观测条件下实现更高效可靠的物体搜索行为。

Abstract: Autonomous object search is challenging for mobile robots operating in indoor environments due to partial observability, perceptual uncertainty, and the need to trade off exploration and navigation efficiency. Classical probabilistic approaches explicitly represent uncertainty but typically rely on handcrafted action-selection heuristics, while deep reinforcement learning enables adaptive policies but often suffers from slow convergence and limited interpretability. This paper proposes a hybrid object-search framework that integrates Bayesian inference with deep reinforcement learning. The method maintains a spatial belief map over target locations, updated online through Bayesian inference from calibrated object detections, and trains a reinforcement learning policy to select navigation actions directly from this probabilistic representation. The approach is evaluated in realistic indoor simulation using Habitat 3.0 and compared against developed baseline strategies. Across two indoor environments, the proposed method improves success rate while reducing search effort. Overall, the results support the value of combining Bayesian belief estimation with learned action selection to achieve more efficient and reliable objectsearch behavior under partial observability.

### [2] [A Mentalistic Interface for Probing Folk-Psychological Attribution to Non-Humanoid Robots](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25646)
*Giulio Pisaneschi,Pierpaolo Serio,Estelle Gerbier,Andrea Dan Ryals,Lorenzo Pollini,Mario G. C. A. Cimino*

Main category: cs.RO

TL;DR: 本研究介绍了一个实验平台，用于研究对非人形机器人的意向状态归因。该平台通过保持行为一致而改变解释框架来探究语言和框架如何影响机器人学中的意向立场采纳。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究动机在于探索语言及表述方式如何影响人们对非人形机器人行为的理解，特别是这些因素如何促使人们采取意向立场去解读机器人行为。

Method: 采用了一种结合了模拟机器人、现实任务环境以及基于大型语言模型的解释层的系统。这些解释层能够以心理主义、目的论或机械论的方式表达相同的行为。

Result: 通过控制行为不变但变化解释框架的方法，此平台提供了一种控制手段来研究语言与框架是如何塑造意向立场在机器人技术中的采纳过程的。

Conclusion: 该实验平台为理解不同解释框架下人类对非人形机器人行为的认知提供了新的视角，有助于深入探讨语言及表述方式在形成意向性理解方面的作用。

Abstract: This paper presents an experimental platform for studying intentional-state attribution toward a non-humanoid robot. The system combines a simulated robot, realistic task environments, and large language model-based explanatory layers that can express the same behavior in mentalistic, teleological, or mechanistic terms. By holding behavior constant while varying the explanatory frame, the platform provides a controlled way to investigate how language and framing shape the adoption of the intentional stance in robotics.

### [3] [Fast-dVLA: Accelerating Discrete Diffusion VLA to Real-Time Performance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25661)
*Wenxuan Song,Jiayi Chen,Shuai Chen,Jingbo Wang,Pengxiang Ding,Han Zhao,Yikai Qin,Xinhu Zheng,Donglin Wang,Yan Wang,Haoang Li*

Main category: cs.RO

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种新的方法，通过在参数空间中解耦辅助任务训练的两个目标（即增强通用能力和适应特定任务的行为分布），来同时实现辅助训练的优点和标准监督微调(SFT)的简洁性。该方法仅需使用两种不同的训练策略使模型在一个小规模的任务集上收敛，然后将由此产生的能力向量与预训练参数合并，形成一个能力增强的元模型。实验结果表明，这种方法在各种机器人任务中都非常有效。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 预训练的VLA模型在标准监督微调过程中往往无法有效提高性能并减少适应成本。虽然一些带有辅助训练目标的高级微调方法能够改善表现并减少收敛步骤数，但它们通常会因为来自辅助任务的额外损失而导致显著的计算开销。因此，研究者们旨在开发一种新方法，在保持较低计算成本的同时提升模型性能。

Method: 研究者们首先识别出辅助任务训练旨在达成的两个主要目标：一是增强模型的一般能力；二是让模型更好地适应特定任务的行为分布。为了以更高效的方式达到这两个目的，他们提出了一个框架，该框架允许在参数空间内分离这两个目标。具体来说，是通过采用两种不同训练策略对同一小规模任务集合进行训练，并利用最终模型参数之间的差异定义为由辅助任务提供的能力向量。这些向量随后被整合进预训练模型参数中，创造出一个增强了能力的新元模型。此外，还引入了轻量级正交正则化损失来进一步优化合并后的模型。

Result: 实验结果证明了所提方法的有效性，不仅在多种机器人任务上表现出色，而且相较于传统的辅助微调基线方案，它还能显著降低所需的计算资源。

Conclusion: 本研究表明，通过巧妙地解耦辅助任务训练的目标并在参数层面进行操作，可以有效地结合辅助训练的优势与标准SFT的简便性，从而为VLA模型提供了一种新的、更高效的改进途径。

Abstract: This paper proposes a novel approach to address the challenge that pretrained VLA models often fail to effectively improve performance and reduce adaptation costs during standard supervised finetuning (SFT). Some advanced finetuning methods with auxiliary training objectives can improve performance and reduce the number of convergence steps. However, they typically incur significant computational overhead due to the additional losses from auxiliary tasks. To simultaneously achieve the enhanced capabilities of auxiliary training with the simplicity of standard SFT, we decouple the two objectives of auxiliary task training within the parameter space, namely, enhancing general capabilities and fitting task-specific action distributions. To deliver this goal, we only need to train the model to converge on a small-scale task set using two distinct training strategies. The difference between the resulting model parameters can then be interpreted as capability vectors provided by auxiliary tasks. These vectors are then merged with pretrained parameters to form a capability-enhanced meta model. Moreover, when standard SFT is augmented with a lightweight orthogonal regularization loss, the merged model attains performance comparable to auxiliary finetuned baselines with reduced computational overhead. Experimental results demonstrate that this approach is highly effective across diverse robot tasks. Project page: https://chris1220313648.github.io/Fast-dVLA/

### [4] [Can Users Specify Driving Speed? Bench2Drive-Speed: Benchmark and Baselines for Desired-Speed Conditioned Autonomous Driving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25672)
*Yuqian Shao,Xiaosong Jia,Langechuan Liu,Junchi Yan*

Main category: cs.RO

TL;DR: 提出了Bench2Drive-Speed，这是一个针对用户指定速度和超车/跟随指令的自动驾驶基准，包含度量标准、数据集和基线。通过调整现有驾驶数据而非仅依赖专家演示来训练模型，实验表明这种方法在遵循目标速度方面有效，但执行超车命令仍具挑战性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的端到端自动驾驶系统未能充分考虑用户的个性化需求，如设定车辆行驶速度或选择是否允许超车等功能。为解决这一问题，并提供一种新的方法以更灵活地控制自动驾驶车辆的行为。

Method: 1. 设计了Bench2Drive-Speed基准，包括用户期望的速度和超车/跟随指示作为显式输入。
2. 开发了Speed-Adherence Score与Overtake Score等量化指标，用以评估策略对用户需求的遵从程度。
3. 构建了CustomizedSpeedDataset，包含2,100个由专家标注的片段，用于探索不同监督策略的有效性。
4. 通过适配常规驾驶数据（将未来帧中的观察速度视为训练的目标速度）而不是收集严格遵守速度要求的专家演示来训练速度条件下的策略。

Result: 研究发现，通过对普通驾驶数据进行适当的重新标注，所训练出的模型在性能上可以与基于专家演示训练的模型相媲美，这表明无需额外复杂的真实世界数据收集即可引入速度监督。此外，尽管实现目标速度跟随不会损害常规驾驶表现，但由于交互行为本身的难度，执行超车命令仍然具有挑战性。

Conclusion: 本工作提出了一种新的框架Bench2Drive-Speed，它允许用户自定义速度条件下的自动驾驶行为，并且证明了可以通过调整已有数据集的方式来有效训练这样的模型，而不需要额外的成本高昂的数据采集过程。不过，在处理复杂的交互行为如超车时，当前方法还存在一定的局限性。

Abstract: End-to-end autonomous driving (E2E-AD) has achieved remarkable progress. However, one practical and useful function has been long overlooked: users may wish to customize the desired speed of the policy or specify whether to allow the autonomous vehicle to overtake. To bridge this gap, we present Bench2Drive-Speed, a benchmark with metrics, dataset, and baselines for desired-speed conditioned autonomous driving. We introduce explicit inputs of users' desired target-speed and overtake/follow instructions to driving policy models. We design quantitative metrics, including Speed-Adherence Score and Overtake Score, to measure how faithfully policies follow user specifications, while remaining compatible with standard autonomous driving metrics. To enable training of speed-conditioned policies, one approach is to collect expert demonstrations that strictly follow speed requirements, an expensive and unscalable process in the real world. An alternative is to adapt existing regular driving data by treating the speed observed in future frames as the target speed for training. To investigate this, we construct CustomizedSpeedDataset, composed of 2,100 clips annotated with experts demonstrations, enabling systematic investigation of supervision strategies. Our experiments show that, under proper re-annotation, models trained on regular driving data perform comparably to on expert demonstrations, suggesting that speed supervision can be introduced without additional complex real-world data collection. Furthermore, we find that while target-speed following can be achieved without degrading regular driving performance, executing overtaking commands remains challenging due to the inherent difficulty of interactive behaviors. All code, datasets and baselines are available at https://github.com/Thinklab-SJTU/Bench2Drive-Speed

</details>
